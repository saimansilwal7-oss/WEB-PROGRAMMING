Nepali Rice and Chicken Curry

What You Need

  • 2 cups rice
  • 500 g chicken
  • 1 onion
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 3 cloves garlic
  • 1 piece of ginger
  • 1 teaspoon turmeric
  • 1 teaspoon cumin
  • 2 tablespoons oil
  • Salt

Steps

  1. Wash the rice and cook it with water.
  2. Heat oil in a pan and fry the onion.
  3. Add garlic, ginger, and tomatoes.
  4. Add the chicken, turmeric, cumin, and salt.
  5. Cook the chicken until it is soft and flavorful.
  6. Add some water and cook the curry for 20–25 minutes.
  7. Warning: Make sure the chicken is fully cooked before eating.
  8. Serve the chicken curry with hot rice.
