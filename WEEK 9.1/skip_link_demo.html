When the skip link receives keyboard focus, it becomes visible. Press Enter to move focus to this main content area.

Why it matters

Keyboard and screen-reader users can bypass repeated navigation and reach the primary content faster.

How to use it

Place the skip link immediately after the opening body tag. Give the main landmark the matching ID. Keep the link visually hidden until it receives focus.
