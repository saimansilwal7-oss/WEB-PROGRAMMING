Why a skip link matters

A skip link lets keyboard and assistive-technology users bypass repeated navigation and reach primary content efficiently.

How to test

  1. Reload this page.
  2. Press Tab once; the skip link should become visible.
  3. Press Enter; the main content should receive focus.
